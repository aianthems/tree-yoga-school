# Tree Yoga School direction

User direction, October 9, 2026: grow Tree Yoga School gradually while AI Anthems remains the primary focus.

## Current foundation

Expand the Champion Tree map from official registers, preserving source provenance, measurements, access labels and approximate geography. Missouri adds 151 register records, bringing coverage to 28 states and 5,502 records. Source dates need not block inclusion when their limitations are clearly described.

## Future community

“Tree Yoga School Big Tree Volunteers”: eventually connect volunteers in each state who can check tree conditions and contribute dated observations. Build toward a connected system of trees, humans and AI. Future reports should complement official registers and clearly distinguish volunteer observations from official champion designations.

## Tree Hugging

Make Tree Hugging a central practice in future Tree Yoga School content, with space for stillness, connection and personal reflection. The founder regards it as a profoundly meditative and healing practice. When writing about benefits, distinguish personal experience from supported research rather than presenting medical promises. Explore Tree Hug events when the community foundation is ready.

These are future directions, not currently launched programs or events.
