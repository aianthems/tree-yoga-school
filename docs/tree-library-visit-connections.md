## Identification details

- Southern live oak: alternate simple unlobed leaves, sometimes toothed juvenile margins, mature blocky/furrowed bark, and seasonal replacement of retained leaves. Spanish moss is not an identification requirement.
- Giant sequoia: scale-like to awl-shaped evergreen foliage matches both needle and scale discovery groups; mature furrowed bark. Native Sierra Nevada groves are distinguished from planted specimens. Size is not treated as proof of age.
- American yellowwood: alternate whole compound leaves; leaflets are not separate leaves for arrangement filters. Unlobed leaflet margins and smooth bark. Heavy flowering is not guaranteed every year. Older botanical name *Cladrastis lutea* is searchable without silently merging source-register species groups.
- Castor aralia: alternate simple lobed leaves and mature furrowed bark. East Asian origin is stated. Spines can occur, so the species practice is explicitly no-touch and requires no trunk embrace or support. *Kalopanax pictus* is a searchable older name.
